About the project

Block Futures is an advanced intelligence platform for crypto derivatives traders, built to uncover what the market hides. By combining real-time whale tracking, hidden liquidity mapping, and AI-driven analysis of futures data and market-moving news, Block Futures turns complex signals into clear, actionable insights.

Instead of reacting to price, users can understand the forces behind it—monitoring large players, identifying key liquidation zones, and spotting momentum before it unfolds. Designed for both active traders and data-driven investors, Block Futures delivers the edge needed to navigate and outperform in fast-moving futures markets.
