Process

From Idea to Running System

Four steps. No setup. No manual commands. Just describe what you want and watch it execute.

01
Describe Your Idea
Type what you want to build in plain English. Pick a template or write your own goal — no technical knowledge required.
02
AI Generates Workflow
Claude Sonnet 4.6 analyzes your idea and generates a structured execution plan with real commands, code, and step explanations.
03
Review and Execute
Inspect commands before they run. Execute step-by-step with full control — the agent walks you through each stage and prompts for confirmation.
04
Idea Becomes Reality
Watch your system come to life in the terminal. Live streaming output, progress tracking, and a complete execution log from start to finish.
